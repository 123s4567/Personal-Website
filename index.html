About

I'm a Ph.D. Scholar and Assistant Professor at Nutan Maharashtra Institute of Engineering and Technology, Pune, with a deep focus on cybersecurity education and digital forensics research.

My work spans embedded systems, UAV forensics, and machine learning—building intelligent frameworks to recover and analyze evidence from drone platforms with proprietary, encrypted data.
