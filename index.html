Unified Forensic Framework for Multi-UAV Evidence Acquisition
To design a unified forensic framework capable of acquiring and analyzing data from multiple UAV models and data formats — addressing proprietary storage, encrypted telemetry, and heterogeneous embedded systems.
02
Machine Learning Workflows for Anomaly Detection & Event Reconstruction
To develop and integrate machine learning–based workflows for anomaly detection, event reconstruction, and behavioral analysis of UAV sensor, telemetry, firmware, and multimedia artifacts.
03
Identification & Mitigation of Anti-Forensic Techniques
To identify and mitigate anti-forensic techniques such as data wiping, encryption, and device tampering — ensuring forensic soundness and legal admissibility of UAV-derived digital evidence.
04
Accuracy, Efficiency & Forensic Soundness Enhancement
To enhance the accuracy, efficiency, and forensic soundness of UAV evidence recovery and analysis — validated through forensic metrics, accuracy benchmarks, and scenario-based testing against real-world UAV investigation cases.
